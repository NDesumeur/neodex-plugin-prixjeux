# Prix des jeux (Game prices)

The games given away on the Epic Games Store, the current Steam sales with prices in euros, and your list of games to watch: a notification arrives when a game drops below the price you chose.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

## Free

The games currently given away by the Epic Games Store, with the offer's end date, and those announced for the following week (marked **Soon**, with their start date). A click opens the game's page.

## Steam sales

The discounts featured by Steam (French store, prices in euros), from the biggest to the smallest: percentage, old and new price. **+ Follow** adds a game to your list.

## My list

1. Search for a Steam game by name, or use **+ Follow** on a sale.
2. Choose your **target price**: the notification arrives when the game drops to that amount or less.
3. The current price and your threshold are shown for each game; you can change the threshold or **remove** the game.

The list is checked every 3 hours, even when the tab is closed. A NeoDex notification arrives when a game drops below your price, and also when a new free game comes out on Epic.

## Data and sync

Your list and target prices are stored by NeoDex, backed up and synced between your PCs when sync is on. Prices are read live from Steam and the Epic Games Store.

## Install

In NeoDex: **NeoStore → Discover → Prix des jeux → Install**.

Building a plugin: see the [NeoDex SDK](https://github.com/NDesumeur/neodex-sdk).

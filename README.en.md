# Prix des jeux (Game prices)

The games given away on the Epic Games Store, the current Steam sales with prices in euros, and your list of games to watch: a notification arrives when a game drops below the price you chose.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

## The lowest price (new in 1.2.0)

In your list, the lowest price seen since you started following and, for Steam, the all-time low (CheapShark): "at its all-time low" when the deal is really worth it.

## Free

The games given away by the **Epic Games Store** this week and next. Below, the games given away **elsewhere** (GOG, Steam, itch.io…), found by GamerPower, with their shop. A **Prime Gaming** card opens the page of games given to Amazon Prime members (Amazon doesn't publish its list).

## Deals

Three shops to pick from: **Steam** and **GOG** (prices in euros, French stores) and **Humble** (prices in dollars, from CheapShark). Each deal's **+ My list** button adds the game to your list in one click. For Humble, the same game's Steam price is followed.

## My list

The search finds **Steam** and **GOG** games. Text is bigger everywhere.

1. Search for a Steam game by name, or use **+ Follow** on a sale.
2. Choose your **target price**: the notification arrives when the game drops to that amount or less.
4. Under the name, the **lowest price**: the lowest NeoDex has seen since you started following the game (in euros), and for Steam games the **all-time low** across all stores (CheapShark, in dollars). "At its all-time low" shows when the current price is within 5 % of it: the deal is really worth it.
3. The current price and your threshold are shown for each game; you can change the threshold or **remove** the game.

The list is checked every 3 hours, even when the tab is closed. A NeoDex notification arrives when a game drops below your price, and also when a new free game comes out on Epic.

## Data and sync

Your list and target prices are stored by NeoDex, backed up and synced between your PCs when sync is on. Prices are read live from Steam and the Epic Games Store.

## Install

In NeoDex: **NeoStore → Discover → Prix des jeux → Install**.

Building a plugin: see the [NeoDex SDK](https://github.com/NDesumeur/neodex-sdk).
